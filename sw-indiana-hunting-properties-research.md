# Secluded Hunting Properties in Southwestern Indiana

*Research compiled: March 13, 2026*

## Top Counties to Explore

The best southwestern Indiana counties for secluded hunting land:

- **Perry County** — Dead-end road properties, very rural
- **Crawford County** — Wooded tracts, close to Hoosier National Forest
- **Gibson County** — Reclaimed coal land, extremely rural with few residences
- **Warrick County** — Timber estates, coal mineral rights potential
- **Spencer County** — Quiet townships, affordable acreage
- **Pike County** — Prime whitetail tracts, reclaimed mining land
- **Sullivan County** — Adjacent to county parks that act as deer sanctuaries

## Notable Listings

### Perry County — Dead-End Road Tract
- Located in Southwestern Perry County, 11 miles from Tell City
- Accessed at the dead-end of Apple Road — very secluded setting
- Rare opportunity for the serious outdoorsman

### Crawford County — 102-Acre Wooded Tract
- 102.02± acres, described as the most secluded and diverse parcel available
- Access off State Road 237
- Ideal hunting and recreational property

### Crawford County — Wooded Tract off SR 66
- All-wooded tract with accessibility, privacy, and natural beauty
- Wide lane off State Rd 66

### 50-Acre Tract Adjoining Hoosier National Forest
- Adjoins 300 acres of the Hoosier National Forest
- Immense opportunity to experience Southern Indiana hunting

### Warrick County — 246-Acre Timberland Estate
- Premier 246± acre recreational and timberland estate
- Located just east of Elberfeld, Indiana

### Gibson County — 353-Acre Secluded Tract
- Very secluded with heavy deer sign
- Crops in fields, large timber/woodland block on south end

### Gibson/Warrick/Pike — 292-Acre Reclaimed Coal Land
- Formerly mined and reclaimed land
- Very rural, almost no houses
- Described as a "recreational paradise"

### Orange County — Off-Grid Cabin Timber Tract
- Off-grid cabin nearly 3/4 mile off County Road 650 W
- Quiet, peaceful, secluded creek bottom setting
- Hardwood ridges full of deer and turkey sign
- Thousands of acres of Hoosier National Forest border the property

### Sullivan County — 69-Acre Sportsman's Paradise
- Near Sullivan, Indiana
- Adjacent to 400-acre Sullivan County Park (no hunting allowed — acts as deer sanctuary)
- Great for hunting mature bucks

### Pike County — 13.87-Acre Whitetail Tract
- Southern Pike County
- Described as a prime whitetail hunting tract

### Spencer County — 40-Acre Tract
- Located in southeastern Clay Township
- North of Newtonville, southwest of Lamar

## Market Overview

- **Average hunting land listing price (statewide):** ~$651,000
- **Southern Indiana average:** ~$329,000 across 17,763 acres available
- Nearly 10,000 acres of hunting land for sale statewide
- Indiana timber industry has $10B+ annual economic impact
- Forests growing at 1.8x the harvest rate (Indiana DNR)
- Mineral rights (coal, limestone) in SW counties can significantly increase land value

## Why Southwestern Indiana for Hidden Hunting Properties

1. **Reclaimed coal land** in Gibson, Warrick, and Pike counties is extremely rural with almost no residences
2. **Hoosier National Forest** (200,000+ acres) borders create massive effective hunting acreage beyond your property boundaries
3. **Dead-end road access** properties in Perry and Crawford counties offer true seclusion
4. Indiana is now famous for producing enormous, heavy-racked whitetail bucks
5. Hunting seasons run October through early January (archery, gun, muzzleloader)
6. Affordable land prices compared to neighboring states
