# Secluded Hunting Properties in Southwestern Indiana

*Research compiled: March 13, 2026*

## Top Counties to Explore

The best southwestern Indiana counties for secluded hunting land:

- **Perry County** — Dead-end road properties, very rural
- **Crawford County** — Wooded tracts, close to Hoosier National Forest
- **Gibson County** — Reclaimed coal land, extremely rural with few residences
- **Warrick County** — Timber estates, coal mineral rights potential
- **Spencer County** — Quiet townships, affordable acreage
- **Pike County** — Prime whitetail tracts, reclaimed mining land
- **Sullivan County** — Adjacent to county parks that act as deer sanctuaries

## Why Southwestern Indiana for Hidden Hunting Properties

1. **Reclaimed coal land** in Gibson, Warrick, and Pike counties is extremely rural with almost no residences
2. **Hoosier National Forest** (200,000+ acres) borders create massive effective hunting acreage beyond your property boundaries
3. **Dead-end road access** properties in Perry and Crawford counties offer true seclusion
4. Indiana is now famous for producing enormous, heavy-racked whitetail bucks
5. Hunting seasons run October through early January (archery, gun, muzzleloader)
6. Affordable land prices compared to neighboring states

## Reclaimed Coal Land — Gibson & Warrick County Deep Dive

### Why Reclaimed Coal Land is Ideal for Hunting

- Coal companies reclaim mined land back to near-original condition
- These areas are extremely rural with almost no houses nearby
- Mix of early-succession habitat, grasslands, timber, and water features
- Creates ideal whitetail browse and bedding cover
- SW Indiana reclaimed land is now known for trophy-class whitetail bucks
